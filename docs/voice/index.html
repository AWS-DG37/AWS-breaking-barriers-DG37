The farmer hears the survey, and still sees it.

Pick a survey that the MEL agent wrote (survey.schema.json). The page reads each question and its choices aloud in an ElevenLabs voice, and the farmer taps the answer. With no tap within 8 seconds, the question plays once more. The answer record follows response.schema.json.
