MEL Survey Agent

The agent reads what FarmerChat learned from a farmer's conversation. It decides if the advice needs a follow-up, writes a short tap-answer survey in the farmer's language, and hands it to a scheduler. Then the team simulates how the farmer answers, failures included.

The contract

Five files pass between the parts. The schemas in json/schemas/ define each one.

FileWhat it carriesWritten byRead by
insightWhat the farmer asked and was toldInsight extractorThe agent
surveyOne to three tap questionsThe agentScheduler
no_surveyWhy an insight gets nothingThe agentReports
schedule_handoffWhen, where and how often to sendThe agentScheduler
responseWhat one survey attempt produced, success or failureThe simulatorDigital Green

A sixth schema, bottom_sheet, is the suggested way for the app and web chat to show a survey: one question per step, with Skip and Close.

Rules the agent starts from

"Use them as the baseline, and improve on them if your evaluation shows a better choice." From the brief, about rules.json.
Survey or not
Direct action, wait and monitor, do not act, plan created and escalate to expert get a survey. General information and unfinished conversations do not. Junk rows get none.
Survey shape
One to three questions: pick one, yes or no, or a scale of up to 10 steps. Prompts under 400 characters, two to ten answers, labels of 20 characters or fewer. One file per language. No new advice and no personal information.
Send window
Opens after the farmer's action window ends, never on the day of the advice. At least two days wide. Sent only from 07:00 to 19:00 India time. Nothing after 31 March, the end of rabi.
Channel order
The farmer's preferred channel, then the channel the check method names, then WhatsApp, then voice for high priority only. Wait 6, 24 or 48 hours between channels for high, normal or low priority.
Priority
High for escalate to expert, high or critical severity, or an emergency. Low for plan created and do not act. Normal for the rest.
Weekly cap
One survey per farmer per 7 days, across every channel. High priority goes out when the cap is full, and still counts.
Expiry
The survey is dropped 2 days after its window closes.

What one answer attempt can produce

Every simulated attempt writes one record. The record says what happened, what to do next, and if it counts toward the weekly cap. A survey counts once the farmer saw it, answered or not.

StatusCodesFor example
completed, partial, negative_outcome3Successes. A negative outcome means the advice failed or harm happened, and it goes to follow-up.
delivery_failed8Push address gone, WhatsApp undelivered, bot blocked, no answer, line busy, call dropped
declined3Not now, opted out, sheet dismissed
invalid_input3Typed text instead of a tap, wrong key, speech not understood
expired2No answer before expiry, notification never opened
stale2A tap after the survey closed or was replaced
duplicate, identity_mismatch2A second answer, or an answer from a number that now belongs to another farmer

Each failure code carries a retry decision: none, retry the same channel, fall back to the next channel, or give up. The full list is in outcome_codes.json.
