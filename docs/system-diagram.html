MEL Survey Agent

The agent reads what FarmerChat learned from a farmer's conversation. It decides if the advice deserves a follow-up, writes a short tap-answer survey in the farmer's language, checks its own output, and hands it to a scheduler. A broadcaster stub and a simulated app then show how farmers answer, failures included.

Sources: the participant pack (brief, rules, schemas and data, 31 October) and Digital Green's Vision and Technical documents (17 October). Formats and rules on this page follow the pack. Scope and priorities follow the Vision document. Where the sources disagree, the page says so.

Definition of done (Vision document)

  • All Must Have features built and tested.
  • The agent runs over the full synthetic insights dataset.
  • Scores against the golden dataset are reported, with at least one improvement round shown.
  • Time per survey and per batch is reported.
  • Invalid or low-scoring surveys are shown not to be scheduled.
  • Survey JSON validates against the agreed schema.
  • Code, infrastructure as code, the evaluation harness and a README are in the open-source repo.

How quality is judged

"Evaluation is the center of this project, not an afterthought." From the Technical document.
  • Method: hill-climb. Run the agent, score against the golden dataset, change prompts or logic, rerun, and show the trend.
  • Dimensions: relevance (does it ask about this farmer's advice), timeliness (send window against the crop calendar and action window), format (valid JSON, tap answers, item count, language), safety (no new advice, no personal information) and speed.
  • Checks: deterministic checks for format and timing. An LLM judge for relevance, on a rubric agreed with Digital Green's MEL team. The document asks teams to confirm if a rubric exists.
  • Language: English is the evaluation baseline. Hindi output is reviewed by Hindi-speaking participants and Digital Green.

What Digital Green expects of the build

From the Technical document. It says to "lean toward Digital Green's stack, but do not get hung up on it."

Language
Python 3.12 or later is required, for the agent, the scheduler and the evaluation harness.
Models
Amazon Bedrock is required. Keep the model choice behind an abstraction so it is easy to swap. A smaller model for eligibility and checks and a larger one for generation is worth exploring. Event staff confirm which models are enabled.
Framework
Google ADK is what Digital Green uses in production. Strands Agents or another framework is acceptable if the REST and JSON contract is met.
Interface
A REST API and a JSON survey object. The suggested libraries are FastAPI, Pydantic or JSON Schema, Boto3 with the Bedrock Converse API, and Streamlit.
Hosting
Bedrock AgentCore Runtime or containers. A containerized agent can later move to Digital Green's Amazon EKS. If a database is needed, use Amazon RDS or Aurora for PostgreSQL.
Infrastructure
AWS CDK or Terraform. All outputs are open source.
Clock
The scheduler and broadcaster read a shared simulated clock, so the demo moves through November to March in minutes. Every scheduled step also has a manual trigger.
Delivery
Do not send real messages to anyone. Deliver to the simulated app only.

Rules the agent starts from

"Use them as the baseline, and improve on them if your evaluation shows a better choice." From the brief, about rules.json.
Survey or not

The brief and rules.json: direct action, wait and monitor, do not act, plan created and escalate to expert get a survey. General information and unfinished conversations do not. Junk rows get none.

The golden set adds more. It expects no survey for 18 rows that this rule calls eligible: critical severity (5), verification.needed false (10), a window that starts after 31 March (2), and a crop already past its calendar (1). In the full input, 1,315 clean insights have an eligible resolution with verification.needed false.

The Vision document adds a principle: "If an insight does not support a meaningful question, generate nothing. A missed survey is better than an irrelevant one."

Survey shape
One to three questions: pick one, yes or no, or a scale of up to 10 steps. Prompts under 400 characters, two to ten answers, labels of 20 characters or fewer. One file per language. No new advice and no personal information.
Send window

Days count from the day of the insight. The window opens at 07:00 and closes at 19:00 India time. It never opens on the day of the advice or before the action window ends. It is at least two days wide. Nothing is sent after 31 March, the end of rabi.

The golden windows break these rules. 88 of the 95 golden windows open at 09:00, not 07:00. 75 of 95 open before the action window ends. All 11 same_day windows are four hours wide, and 7 of them open on the day of the advice.

Channel order
The farmer's preferred channel, then the channel the check method names, then WhatsApp, then voice for high priority only. Wait 6, 24 or 48 hours between channels for high, normal or low priority.
Priority

High for escalate to expert, high or critical severity, or an emergency. Low for plan created and do not act. Normal for the rest.

The golden set disagrees on critical. It gives all 6 critical rows no survey, with the reason critical_needs_human_call.

Weekly cap
One survey per farmer per 7 days, across every channel. High priority goes out when the cap is full, and still counts.
Expiry
The survey is dropped 2 days after its window closes.

What one answer attempt can produce

Every simulated attempt writes one record. The record says what happened, what to do next, and if it counts toward the weekly cap. The brief says a survey counts "once the farmer has seen it, answered or not."

StatusCodesFor example
completed, partial, negative_outcome3Successes. A negative outcome means the advice failed or harm happened, and it goes to follow-up.
delivery_failed8Push address gone, WhatsApp undelivered, bot blocked, no answer, line busy, call dropped
declined3Not now, opted out, sheet dismissed
invalid_input3Typed text instead of a tap, wrong key, speech not understood
expired2No answer before expiry, notification never opened
stale2A tap after the survey closed or was replaced
duplicate, identity_mismatch2A second answer, or an answer from a number that now belongs to another farmer

Each failure code carries a retry decision: none, retry the same channel, fall back to the next channel, or give up. The full list is in outcome_codes.json.

One code breaks the counting rule. outcome_codes.json counts push_not_opened toward the cap, but its meaning says the sheet never opened, so the farmer never saw the survey.

Questions to take to Digital Green

Rules

  • Critical severity. The golden set gives critical cases no survey and a human call. rules.json gives them a high-priority survey. Which one wins?
  • No check needed. Is verification.needed false a deliberate "no survey" signal? It decides 10 golden rows and 1,315 clean input rows.
  • Crop past its calendar. One golden row gets no survey because the crop's last calendar stage has ended. What exactly triggers this rule?
  • Timing. Which governs: the brief's table, rules.json, or the golden windows (09:00 starts, same-day windows)?
  • Cap. Does push_not_opened count toward the weekly cap?

Contract

  • No-survey reasons. no_survey.schema.json allows two reason codes. The golden set uses seven. Which list is right?
  • Conditional questions. The golden set shows "If not, what stopped you?" only when q1 is not "Yes". survey.schema.json has no field for a condition. How is this expressed?
  • Resolution types. The Vision document names three (direct action, indirect action, wait and watch). The pack uses seven. Confirm that the pack schemas are the agreed contract.
  • Grouping. The schema allows up to five source insights per survey. Do you want several insights for one farmer combined into one survey?

Evaluation and event

  • Rubric. Does the MEL team have a relevance rubric for the LLM judge?
  • Golden set updates. The golden set is version 1 and can change during the event. How will updates reach the teams?
  • Models. Which Bedrock models are enabled in the Workshop Studio accounts, and in which region?

Still open for the team

Out of scope for the MVP

From the Vision document: production FarmerChat, broadcaster and app integration, real survey delivery, AgMCP servers and live data, the NightSync, SeasonPlanner and FarmWatch agents, languages beyond Hindi and English, regions beyond Bihar, replacing RCTs, and delivery at scale. All data is synthetic.
